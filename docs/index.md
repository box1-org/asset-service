# asset-service

Represent connected industrial equipment at multiple sites.

## Overview

Represent connected industrial equipment at multiple sites: a believable high-volume async workload and an independent incident source, not a thin synonym for Kafka. The service also carries a load and chaos console scoped to telemetry. The prompt proposed `box1-ops-telemetry`; the service runs as `box1-org/asset-service`.
